Quick way to introduce it:

“I put together a small idea for the counter. People could tap a sticker or scan a QR code and get straight to the voter services we answer questions about all day. It doesn't take any personal information or replace staff; it just makes the official resources easier to find. If it seems useful, maybe we could pilot one sign and see how people respond.”

Demo order:
1. Show the visitor page on your phone.
2. Tap Check Registration or Find Polling Place.
3. Switch to Spanish.
4. Show the counter sign and explain that both NFC and QR open the same maintained page.
5. Ask for feedback; don't present it as a completed county program.
